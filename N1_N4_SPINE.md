## Logical progression

### N1 — membership

N1 decides which environmental dimensions belong in an interpretable realized-niche representation. Predictive adequacy is a guardrail, not the scientific endpoint. Fitting evidence and answer-check evidence remain separated.

### N2 — projection and thickness

N2 asks how much ecological state-space structure is lost when a multidimensional niche is collapsed to a planar map. Vertical, temporal and structural axes may make equal x-y area ecologically unequal.

### N3 — realization

N3 begins after local states are supported. It asks where those states can actually become realized once accessibility, barriers and alternative transition worlds are retained instead of collapsed to one assumed route.

### N4 — justified inquiry

N4 converts the remaining knowledge and uncertainty into a bounded next-observation decision: where field effort can be justified next.

The handoff is conceptual, not a requirement that every analysis run all four repositories. Each chapter retains its own estimand and validation boundary.

## Non-retroactivity rule

A downstream result cannot be used to retune or rescue an already opened upstream endpoint. Existing scientific closures, frozen denominators, hard stops and negative/conditional results remain authoritative. The N1–N4 spine organizes interpretation and future interfaces; it does not rewrite completed evidence.
