# Four-chapter research program

Program ID: `niche-to-survey-four-chapter-v1`

The chapter order is fixed:

1. **SDMR — environmental niche-driver selection**: which environmental dimensions best define an interpretable realized niche, using separate model-pool and sealed answer-check occurrence roles.
2. **ODSP — multidimensional niche geometry**: how much niche structure is hidden by the x-y projection; quantify vertical, temporal and structural niche thickness.
3. **EOG — distributional worlds and reachability**: given supported local states and declared transition worlds, which distributional worlds remain compatible and what states are possible/robust/unresolved/reachable.
4. **ACSP — survey action**: where field effort should be directed next.

## Chapter 4 scientific center

ACSP is the **action / survey-candidate** chapter. It does not choose the environmental drivers of the niche, measure niche thickness, or infer one true historical route. It converts frozen occurrence-conditioned support into bounded places worth surveying.

The core question is:

> **Given the information and support available before a survey, which bounded candidate patches concentrate future detections better than an appropriate same-size reference?**

The validated Japanese product remains the frozen 2.5% robust-support candidate tier aggregated into bounded patches. Its supported claim is enrichment of held-out occurrences at the declared regional screening scale, not occupancy probability, exact-site presence, route efficiency or universal global optimality.

### Fixed Chapter-4 goal

Provide a prospectively validated bridge from ecological knowledge to field action: a small, auditable set of candidate patches or priorities that a field researcher can actually inspect, while retaining abstention when the input evidence is insufficient.

### Current boundary

The current ACSP paper is scientifically closed at the validated Japanese-domain product plus failed/conditional country generalization and provider-supply abstention. This chapter map does not authorize same-cohort rescue or a new global confirmation for the present paper. Remaining current-paper work is manuscript/package completion.

## Interface to upstream chapters

ACSP may consume support from multiple sources. In future separately validated applications:

- SDMR can provide a driver-selected environmental niche representation;
- ODSP can provide axis-resolved niche thickness or temporal/vertical constraints;
- EOG can provide robust/possible world support or hypothesis-discriminating structure.

None of these inputs are mandatory for the already validated Japanese ACSP product, and downstream survey results may not retroactively retune upstream chapters.
