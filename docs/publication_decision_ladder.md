# ACSP publication decision ladder

Status checked: 2026-08-28.

This document is a **publication-strategy boundary only**. It does not change the validated Japanese candidate-patch contract, the frozen 2.5% support tier, the 1 km aggregation rule, any prospective provider/evaluability protocol, or any favorable/null/adverse/unavailable/STOP interpretation.

## Current scientific anchor

ACSP already has a validated narrow product:

> In the fixed Japanese 12-region validation frame, the frozen 2.5% robust-support candidate tier enriches held-out occurrences relative to same-size random selection at the 10 km regional screening scale across the tested plant and animal cohort.

The country-framed extension has not been promoted. Its reserved replication and completely fresh 48-taxon confirmation retained positive mean lift among evaluable taxa but failed preregistered generality/evaluability gates.

The provider-eligible observability successor is a separate prospective question. Its first activation stopped during historical/provider eligibility before a complete authoritative 96-frame artifact and before 2021-2025 heldout outcomes were opened. That execution is **not evaluable for the hypothesis** and is not a scientific negative.

## Publication principle

ACSP does **not** need a global positive result before the validated Japanese product can be published. Journal choice depends on how much of the full ordinary-use chain is independently supported:

`taxon -> geographic frame -> observability/evaluability -> candidate patches -> explicit abstention`

A failed generalization attempt must be reported as such rather than hidden or repaired until positive.

## Route A — highest-challenge applied/general ecology route

A high-selectivity challenge such as **Nature Ecology & Evolution** or **Journal of Applied Ecology** is justified only if ACSP grows beyond a candidate-patch algorithm into a broadly supported ecological survey principle.

### Nature Ecology & Evolution trigger

Consider one editorial challenge only if fresh heterogeneous evidence supports a general result such as:

> Biodiversity survey performance depends not only on predicted ecological support, but on prospectively identifiable observability/evaluability structure that determines where a survey algorithm can validly make decisions.

This requires more than a favorable country-frame score. It should include:

- a prospectively frozen, outcome-blind framing/evaluability procedure;
- fresh heterogeneous taxa/regions beyond the original Japanese frame;
- complete denominator accounting including abstentions;
- evidence that the observability/evaluability layer materially changes the validity or transferability of survey recommendations;
- no claim of occupancy probability or universal optimality.

### Journal of Applied Ecology trigger

Prefer **Journal of Applied Ecology** if the strongest advance is practical field decision support, especially if an independent prospective field test shows that ACSP-guided survey allocation improves detection/recovery under comparable effort, cost, or field opportunity relative to a defensible conventional strategy.

Without real management/survey-practice evidence, do not force an applied-management narrative merely for journal prestige.

## Route B — Methods in Ecology and Evolution

Use **Methods in Ecology and Evolution** when an end-to-end ordinary-use method has been independently confirmed beyond the fixed Japanese frame, including the ability to abstain when the evidence supply is inadequate.

A competitive MEE version should ideally support:

- deterministic outcome-blind geographic framing or an equally explicit frame contract;
- prospectively testable observability/evaluability;
- the unchanged validated candidate-patch core;
- complete failure/abstention accounting;
- fresh heterogeneous confirmation of the complete procedure;
- clear separation from route, budget, travel-day, occupancy-probability, or exact-site optimization claims.

A favorable observability hypothesis alone does not automatically promote the candidate-patch product globally. The complete procedure still needs its own valid fresh confirmation.

## Route C — Ecological Informatics

**Ecological Informatics is the strong default route under the current boundary.**

This route is appropriate if the final evidence remains:

- Japanese candidate-patch product independently validated;
- global/country-framed transfer positive among evaluable taxa but not fully generalizable under preregistered gates;
- observability/evaluability incomplete, conditional, null, adverse, or technically unavailable;
- explicit abstention and provider/supply accounting treated as part of the computational ecology contribution.

The paper should then center on a narrow, honest computational claim:

> ACSP converts occurrence-conditioned environmental support into bounded survey candidate patches with independently validated enrichment in its declared Japanese frame, while prospective transfer experiments reveal the separate observability/evaluability constraints that limit automatic geographic generalization.

The failed generalization gates are evidence about applicability, not embarrassing side results.

## Provider observability outcome mapping

For the current prospective observability line:

- **favorable:** supports the observability component only; proceed to a separately frozen complete end-to-end transfer test before claiming global ACSP validation;
- **null:** the historical count score does not add the preregistered prospective ranking signal; retain validated Japan and treat global ordinary-use framing as conditional/unresolved;
- **adverse:** narrow the observability route; do not reinterpret the candidate-patch core as failed;
- **unavailable / pre-heldout STOP:** no hypothesis result; preserve the supply/evaluability failure as methods-integrity evidence;
- **technical abort after an exact scientific object is frozen:** do not create a new scientific cohort merely to obtain a cleaner result unless a prospectively new successor contract is justified independently.

## Development stop rule

Do not make "global positive" the publication completion criterion.

The current paper can close once one of these is true:

1. a complete fresh end-to-end generalization test reaches a valid terminal decision; or
2. the active observability/evaluability line reaches a scientifically interpretable terminal boundary and the validated-Japan plus failed/conditional generalization story is fully auditable.

After that point, stop adding ranking, route, budget, travel-day, or generic SDM features to increase perceived scope. Those are different products/questions.
