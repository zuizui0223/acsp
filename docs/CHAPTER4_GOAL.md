# Chapter 4 goal — ACSP

Program ID: `niche-to-survey-four-chapter-v1`

**Chapter 4 = survey action.**

## Scientific goal

Convert pre-survey ecological information into a small, auditable set of bounded candidate patches where field effort is worth spending. The chapter asks where to look next, not what the niche is or which historical process generated the present distribution.

## Current validated product

The validated Japanese-domain path remains the frozen 2.5% robust-support candidate tier aggregated into bounded patches. Its evidence supports enrichment of held-out occurrences relative to same-size random selection at the declared screening scale.

## Current implementation goal

The current chapter manuscript is scientifically closed. Remaining work is:

1. finalize the Ecological Informatics manuscript and figures;
2. present the validated Japanese product as the positive core result;
3. retain the country-framed replication/generalization failures and provider-supply abstention as explicit boundary evidence;
4. package the minimal candidate-patch interface for reproducible use.

## Future interface goal

A later separately validated ACSP programme may consume richer upstream products:

- Chapter 1 / SDMR environmental niche-driver support;

- Chapter 3 / EOG robust/possible world support.

Those future interfaces may not be used to retroactively expand the current validated claim.

## Boundaries

- Do not claim occupancy probability or exact-site presence from candidate patches.
- Do not infer a historical route; that is outside Chapter 4.
- Do not reopen same-cohort rescue or tune the current paper toward a global positive result.
- Route/budget optimization is not part of the validated candidate-patch product.
