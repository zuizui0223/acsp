# Does topology-informed location choice improve equal-effort *Cirsium* discovery?

**Status (16 October 2026):** method-design and synthetic implementation test **only**. No fresh cohort identities or private range-sector polygons have been pinned. No biological field outcomes, real candidate-site allocations, prospective searches, effect estimates, or discovery claims have been opened. This is **not** a new fourth arm of the already frozen three-arm fresh-SENTINEL plan and does **not** amend the independently validated Japanese robust-patch algorithm.

## Motivation: what is known, and what is not

The original October 8 PR #256 source-verified Cirsium 100-m retained-support topology audit, and the October 15 PR #273 post hoc arithmetic summary, showed that 1-km complete-link patch diameter does **not** ensure connectivity within a retained high-support mask. Internally disconnected patches comprised 320/573 in the wetland configuration, 3,292/3,883 in the alpine configuration and 278/654 in each of the two open-grassland configurations. Those four runs represent only **three configured structural families**; the two grassland outcomes are not independent evidence. The patches were already derived from terrain and source-conditioned ecological structure; none of their component boundaries demonstrates actual habitat barriers, occupancy, or access.

**New prospective hypothesis (not tested):** Within a privately verified, source-complete, originally frozen patch whose retained 100-m cells contain two or more eight-neighbour (Moore) connected fragments, selecting a site from the *largest connected fragment* increases the probability of a verified current focal-taxon occurrence after matched search effort, compared with a site selected using the geometry of *all* cells without considering connected fragments. This is a **downstream site-interpretation comparison**, not a patch-generation change.

## Two exactly defined counterfactual site rules

Both strategies are given the **same exact set of original retained 100-m cells** inside the same original 1-km complete-link patch. Neither strategy may add a cell, move a patch, use prospective detections, or turn source-indeterminate cells into absences.

1. `LARGEST_MOORE8_COMPONENT_MEDOID_V1` (topology): Reconstruct eight-neighbour connected components inside the patch. Choose the component with the largest cell count; ties use the smallest `(grid_row, grid_col)` pair. Choose the observed retained cell minimizing the exact integer sum of squared distances to every member of that selected component; ties are lexicographic by `(row, col)`.
2. `ALL_RETAINED_PATCH_CELLS_MEDOID_V1` (geometry-only comparator): Ignore component membership and choose the retained cell minimizing the same squared-distance sum across **all** cells of the original patch, with the same lexicographic tie break.

Both candidate sites are original retained cells. If both rules select the same cell, **retain the patch** and report this overlap; do not discard it after looking at results. The trial's sampling frame consists only of patches that are internally disconnected **before field outcomes**. All other patches are reported as ineligible by design. The source SHA, selected candidate IDs and site geometry must later be pinned privately before any actual visits.

## Assignment and effort (design, not a field schedule)

For an independently declared taxon/sector, sort its disconnected patches by SHA-256 of the fixed protocol salt, cohort code and exact private patch reference. Assign the first even number alternately to topology and geometry; when there is an odd remainder, put exactly one pre-outcome hashed patch into a non-biological **balance reserve**, with no candidate search. This yields exactly equal scheduled *unique patch* counts and planned person-minutes within each taxon. A cohort with fewer than two eligible patches cannot make a between-arm comparison; retain that non-evaluable cohort in its original frozen registry without replacing it for favorable outcomes.

Each assigned patch gets **one** preselected counterfactual location, not both, to limit within-patch search contamination. At that location, keep the independently frozen standardized effort of **three 30-minute searches by one observer**, or **90 `PERSON_MINUTES_V1` per unique assigned candidate**. The field schedule (dates, observer, permissions, actual coordinates, candidate order and exact cumulative effort) cannot be generated or exposed until the separately frozen private geometry, newly selected disjoint taxon registry, access and permission eligibility, public source-hash receipt and full schedule pin exist. The design-stage code **never writes a real field schedule**.

The existing fresh-SENTINEL main comparison remains strictly: structural-within-coverage vs coverage-only vs Morton spatial balance. Its original endpoint, arm symmetry, per-taxon primary contrast, candidate-order prefixes and future private schedule are not edited or treated as this trial's fourth arm. Reusing CIR02/CIR06/CIR12/CIR13 is explicitly **previously exposed development**, even if their future field outcomes are unopened. A distinct, outcome-blind identity-frozen taxon/sector registry is necessary before any independent claim.

## Endpoint and intended estimand, still blocked

Inherit the pre-existing biological states: `SEARCH_COMPLETED_DETECTED_VERIFIED` is a verified current-occurrence success, `SEARCH_COMPLETED_NOT_DETECTED` is a completed resolved non-detection (not biological absence), and unresolved identity, denied access or permissions, phenology failure and incomplete searches are **not** recoded as non-detections. A candidate succeeds if at least one of its three scheduled completed visits has a verified detection. If no verified detection occurs, it enters the resolved non-detection denominator only when all scheduled visits are resolved completed non-detections. Report attempts, lost/unevaluable visits, true completed-person-minutes, and candidate overlap **by arm and taxon**.

The conditional contrast of interest is, for each preregistered taxon, the resolved-candidate success proportion in topology **minus** the analogous geometry-only proportion. However, a positive conditional difference is **not automatically a causal field-efficiency effect** if access, phenology, permissions or search completeness differ across arms. Mandatory evaluability and attrition reporting, the complete pre-outcome schedule and outcome-blind cohort enrollment are prerequisites to any causal interpretation. Cross-taxon weights, uncertainty procedure, design sample size and formal positive/negative primary decision rules remain **unfrozen** until the disjoint biological cohort and capacity are registered; no post hoc favorable subset is permitted.

## Source, privacy and falsifiability gates

The design parser accepts only private patch membership cells of the form `<unit>_r<signed-grid-row>_c<signed-grid-column>` on the original 100-m integer lattice, rejects duplicates and any patch whose member diameter exceeds 1 km, and works with the exact original membership. **Future actual input requires upstream source SHA proof** rather than merely syntactically valid IDs; the current preview is *not* an authenticated original-source replay. Private previews contain selected IDs and assignments only in memory, and public previews include aggregate counts and a single full-assignment checksum, not sites, cells or coordinates. The actual outcome ledger must remain private.

Fail-closed gates before any real field analysis: new disjoint taxon/sector registry frozen, original patch-membership and source-complete receipts pinned, both strategies and paired pre-field access/permissions assessed without outcomes, any shared selected site retained, candidate assignment and three-repeat equal-effort schedule pinned, and complete biological positives, non-detections and access/phenology failures recorded. If any gate fails, classify the trial **NOT READY / NOT EVALUABLE**, not as a negative species result.
